# Kinkan: a modified version of mikan

Kinkan is a modified version of mikan, made by GetsuNoKaze:
<https://github.com/GetsuNoKaze/kinkan>.

It is not mikan and not an official mikan release, and it is not made, checked or
supported by the mikan project. The official mikan is only
[Miroshka000/mikan](https://github.com/Miroshka000/mikan). Questions about Kinkan go to
[Kinkan's issues](https://github.com/GetsuNoKaze/kinkan/issues), not to mikan's chat or
issues.
